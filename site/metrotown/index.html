The City of Burnaby expects all property owners and managers to maintain clear, safe conditions on their properties during winter weather. This includes clearing snow and ice from driveways, sidewalks, pathways, and parking areas. Under Burnaby's property maintenance bylaws, property owners are responsible for removing snow and ice within specific timeframes after precipitation to maintain public safety. Failure to maintain safe snow and ice removal conditions can result in city citations, fines, and significant liability if someone is injured on your property due to hazardous conditions. Burnaby takes property maintenance seriously, and winter snow removal compliance is a critical component of meeting city standards. Metrotown properties in particular, given their high foot traffic and commercial density, face heightened scrutiny of winter maintenance. Understanding Metrotown Winter Weather Patterns

Metrotown's winter weather creates unique challenges that vary significantly from year to year, making professional snow management essential for property safety.

Typical Metrotown Winter Conditions

Metrotown experiences a Pacific Northwest winter climate with temperatures typically ranging from -2°C to 6°C during peak winter months (December through February). While the Metrotown area doesn't receive heavy snowfall every year, when snow does arrive, it can be sudden and significant, creating hazardous conditions requiring immediate attention. Average annual snowfall in the Metrotown area ranges from 20-40 cm, though extreme winters can bring substantially greater accumulation that impacts the busy commercial district.

Freeze-Thaw Cycles & Ice Accumulation

Metrotown's location and Lower Mainland geography create frequent freeze-thaw cycles, especially during shoulder seasons (November and March). These temperature fluctuations lead to dangerous ice buildup on driveways, parking lots, sidewalks, and retail areas. The ice that forms is particularly hazardous because it's difficult to detect and causes slipping hazards that lead to serious injuries. Strategic de-icing and salt treatment are essential during these periods to maintain safe Metrotown properties.

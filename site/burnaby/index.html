Property Owner Responsibilities

Legal Requirement: Property owners must clear snow and ice from sidewalks on their property by 10 a.m. each day during winter months under Burnaby municipal bylaws.
  • Clear all sidewalks adjacent to your property daily
  • Shovel snow onto your property, not the street or into traffic
  • Remove ice and apply sand or salt for traction
  • Clear storm drains and grates
  • Maintain clear pathways for pedestrians
  • Ensure wheelchair and mobility device access
  • Keep driveways clear to avoid blocking street access

Burnaby Snow Removal Bylaws & Penalties

The City of Burnaby enforces strict snow removal bylaws under the Property Maintenance Bylaw. Understanding these regulations is essential for all property owners in Metrotown, Brentwood, and all Burnaby neighborhoods.

Key Bylaw Requirements

  • Sidewalk Clearing: Must be cleared by 10 a.m. daily during winter
  • Snow Placement: Snow must be placed on your property only, not on street or neighbor's property
  • Seasonal Duration: Requirements apply whenever snow or ice is present
  • Accessibility: Clear pathways must be safe for pedestrians and wheelchair users
  • Street Parking: Do not block storm drains or city access with snow piles
  • Driveways: Clear snow from driveway approaches to street

Fines & Penalties for Non-Compliance

Violation Fine Amount
Snow/ice on sidewalk after 10 a.m. $250
Snow remains over 24 hours $500
Snow blocks street or storm drain $500
Repeat violations (2nd offense) $750
Repeat violations (3+ times) Up to $1,000
Dangerous condition created $1,000-$2,000

Enforcement & City Actions

If property owners fail to comply with bylaws, the City of Burnaby may:

  • Issue violation notices with 24-hour compliance deadlines
  • Perform snow removal at owner's expense (recovery through city billing)
  • Levy ongoing fines for continued violations
  • Place liens on properties for unpaid snow removal costs
  • Report violations to property insurance companies
  • Take legal action for serious violations

Report Snow & Ice Issues to Burnaby

Use Burnaby's reporting system to report uncleared sidewalks, hazardous icy conditions, and blocked drains on public property. This helps keep neighborhoods safe and ensures code enforcement, especially in busy areas like Metrotown and Brentwood.

How to Report

Call Burnaby

Phone: 604-294-7944
Hours: Available during business hours; emergency line 24/7
Cost: Free service for Burnaby residents

Online Reporting

Report online through the City of Burnaby website:

City of Burnaby Website

What to Report

  • Uncleared sidewalks in front of homes or businesses
  • Hazardous icy conditions on public streets and walkways
  • Blocked or frozen storm drains
  • Snow piles blocking visibility or traffic flow
  • Inadequate city snow removal on priority routes
  • Dangerous ice on wheelchair accessible paths
  • Blocked transit stop access (SkyTrain, bus routes)

Response & Enforcement

Burnaby's bylaw enforcement team will:

  • Investigate reported violations within 24-48 hours (weather permitting)
  • Issue violation notices to non-compliant property owners
  • Allow 24 hours for property owners to clear snow
  • Perform city snow removal at owner's expense if non-compliance continues
  • Continue enforcement and fines for repeat violations

Burnaby School Closures Due to Winter Weather

Winter weather can occasionally force school closures in Burnaby. Here's how to stay informed about closures for Burnaby's school district.

Burnaby School District (SD 41)

Decision Time: Closure decisions are made by 6:30 a.m. on the day in question.
  • Largest school district serving Burnaby
  • Covers approximately 55,000 students
  • Serves all of Burnaby including Metrotown, Brentwood, Burnaby North, Burnaby South

How to Get Notifications:

  • Check SD 41 website: www.sd41.bc.ca
  • School closure page: SD 41 Closure Information
  • Enable ParentAlert text/email notifications
  • Follow on social media: @BurnabySchools
  • Check local news stations (CityNews, CTV, Global)
  • Call your child's school directly for confirmation

Metrotown & Brentwood Area Schools

Schools in Metrotown and Brentwood areas are served by Burnaby School District (SD 41). These include elementary, middle, and secondary schools throughout the commercial and residential centers.

Winter Closure Preparedness Tips

  • Update emergency contact information with your school immediately
  • Sign up for ParentAlert and all notification systems
  • Don't assume schools are open (only closures/delays are announced)
  • Have a backup childcare plan ready
  • Check closure announcements by 7 a.m. on winter mornings
  • Monitor weather forecasts the night before
  • Verify with school website if uncertain

Burnaby 10-Year Snow & Ice Climate Averages

Burnaby experiences moderate winter weather, receiving more snow than coastal Vancouver but less than inland Surrey or elevated North Shore areas. Understanding typical conditions helps you prepare effectively for Metrotown, Brentwood, and all neighborhoods.

Annual Snow Statistics

Metric Average
Annual snowfall 45-65 cm (18-26 inches)
Snow days per year 12-18 days with measurable snow
Days with snow on ground 25-45 days with 1cm+ accumulation
Heaviest month January (3-4 snow days, 100-150mm)
Second heaviest February (2-3 snow days, 80-120mm)

Monthly Snow Breakdown

  • December: Variable, 1-2 snow days (20-40mm)
  • January: Heavy snow season (3-4 snow days, 100-150mm average)
  • February: Significant snow (2-3 snow days, 80-120mm)
  • March: Transition month (0-2 snow days, melting occurs)
  • April: Minimal snow (trace to 5mm)
  • May-November: Essentially no snow

Freezing & Ice Days

75-95 days

Average number of days with freezing temperatures annually

Burnaby experiences freeze-thaw cycles, creating icy conditions even with light snow. This is particularly common in January and February. Regional Variations Within Burnaby

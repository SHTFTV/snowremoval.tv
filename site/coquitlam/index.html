Understanding Coquitlam Winter Weather

Coquitlam's winter weather patterns vary considerably from year to year, making professional snow management essential.

Typical Coquitlam Winter Conditions

Coquitlam experiences a Pacific Northwest winter climate with temperatures typically ranging from -5°C to 5°C during peak winter months. While the city doesn't always receive heavy snowfall, when snow does arrive, it can be sudden and significant, creating hazardous conditions requiring immediate attention.

Ice Accumulation Challenges

Temperature fluctuations in Coquitlam often lead to freeze-thaw cycles, creating dangerous ice buildup on driveways and walkways. This ice formation is particularly hazardous because it's difficult to detect and causes slipping hazards that can lead to injuries.

Plan Winter Entrances With Pets and Plants in Mind

Published October 18, 2026 · PlowWow editorial

Map doors used by residents, visitors and people walking dogs. Note planting beds next to cleared paths and places where runoff collects. Share these details with the provider before choosing treatment material

Identify shared routes

Map doors used by residents, visitors and people walking dogs. Note planting beds next to cleared paths and places where runoff collects. Share these details with the provider before choosing treatment materials. Do not assume a product described in general terms is appropriate for every surface or setting.

Original PlowWow illustrative brand animation. Not a record of a customer job.

Read the actual label

Check the product’s application directions, storage requirements and stated restrictions. Keep the packaging available for staff. Avoid making blanket pet-safe or environmentally harmless claims. If an exposure concern arises, seek advice from an appropriate professional using the exact product information.

Original PlowWow illustrative brand animation. Not a record of a customer job.

Keep entrances orderly

Remove slush and accumulations through the agreed clearing process. Provide an appropriate place to wipe footwear inside without creating a trip hazard or blocking the door. Ask users to follow the property’s guidance. Keep bags and tools out of reach of children and animals.

Original PlowWow illustrative brand animation. Not a record of a customer job.

Review where material travels

Observe runoff and material tracked into the building after use. Record excessive buildup or repeated wetting for the property manager and provider. Adjust the plan through the responsible people, following product directions. Read the drainage guide when a treatment area remains repeatedly wet.

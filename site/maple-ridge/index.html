🌨️ Chilliwack Snow Removal Guide

Chilliwack experiences more significant winter weather than many Lower Mainland communities due to its Fraser Valley location and higher elevation.

Chilliwack Budget & Services

  • Annual Budget: $2.8 Million for snow and ice removal
  • Priority Areas: Main arterial roads, Williams Lake Road, downtown sidewalks
  • Key Service: 24/7 road treatment during winter weather events
  • Population: 88,000+

Chilliwack School Districts

  • Chilliwack School District (SD 33)
  • Closure decisions by 6:30 a.m.
  • Website: sd33.bc.ca
  • Weather procedures: Weather Closure Information

Chilliwack Climate Data (10-Year Average)

  • Annual snowfall: 120-150 cm (47-59 inches) - significantly higher than coastal areas
  • Snow days per year: 25-30 days with measurable snow
  • Days with snow on ground: 60-90 days
  • Coldest month: January (-1.2°C average)
  • Heaviest snow months: December, January, February
  • Notable: Chilliwack receives 3-4x more snow than Vancouver

Report Issues to Chilliwack 311

  • Phone: 311 or 604-792-0221
  • Online: Contact city website
  • Report: Uncleared sidewalks, hazardous road conditions, blocked drains

❄️ Abbotsford Snow Removal Guide

Abbotsford, the largest city in the Fraser Valley, faces regular winter weather with significant snowfall typical of the region.

Abbotsford Budget & Services

  • Annual Budget: $3.2 Million for winter maintenance and snow removal
  • Priority Areas: Highway 1, Highstreet, Mount Lehman, transit corridors
  • Key Service: Pre-treatment and rapid response during snowfall
  • Population: 142,000+

Abbotsford School Districts

  • Abbotsford School District (SD 34)
  • Closure decisions by 6:30 a.m.
  • Website: abbyschools.ca
  • Weather information: Check district website and news outlets
  • Mission School District (SD 75) - serves parts of Abbotsford area

Abbotsford Climate Data (10-Year Average)

  • Annual snowfall: 100-130 cm (39-51 inches)
  • Snow days per year: 22-28 days with measurable snow
  • Days with snow on ground: 55-85 days
  • Coldest month: January (-1.5°C average)
  • Heaviest snow months: December, January, February
  • Notable: More moderate than higher elevations (Chilliwack)

Report Issues to Abbotsford

  • Phone: 311 or 604-864-4000
  • Online: City of Abbotsford website
  • Report: Road hazards, uncleared sidewalks, drainage issues

⛄ Maple Ridge & Pitt Meadows Snow Removal Guide

Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows experience moderate to heavy winter weather with variable snowfall depending on elevation and proximity to the mountains.

Maple Ridge Budget & Services

  • Annual Budget: $2.5 Million for snow and winter maintenance
  • Priority Areas: Dewdney Trunk Road, 224th Street, main arterials
  • Elevation Challenge: Higher elevations see more snow accumulation
  • Population (Maple Ridge): 92,000+

Pitt Meadows Services

  • Annual Budget: $1.2 Million for winter maintenance
  • Priority Areas: Pitt River Road, Central Boulevard
  • Population: 20,000+

School Districts

  • Maple Ridge School District (SD 42)
  • Closure decisions by 6:30 a.m.
  • Website: sd42.ca
  • Weather closures: Check district website and SchoolMessenger

Maple Ridge & Pitt Meadows Climate Data (10-Year Average)

  • Annual snowfall: 80-120 cm (31-47 inches) - varies with elevation
  • Snow days per year: 18-25 days with measurable snow
  • Days with snow on ground: 40-70 days
  • Coldest month: January (-1.0°C average)
  • Heaviest snow months: January, February, March
  • Notable: Lower elevations (Pitt Meadows) see less snow than higher areas (Mission area)

Report Issues

  • Maple Ridge Phone: 604-463-3311
  • Pitt Meadows Phone: 604-465-5008
  • Both Cities: Report road hazards and sidewalk issues to city public works

🌨️ Tsawwassen Snow Removal Guide

Tsawwassen, the southernmost community in the Lower Mainland, features mild winters with minimal snowfall due to its southern coastal location.

Tsawwassen Service Information

  • Municipality: Part of Delta (see Delta guide for budget)
  • Winter Conditions: Mildest in Lower Mainland; snow is rare
  • Key Feature: Tsawwassen ferry terminal requires year-round access
  • Population: 15,000+ (part of Delta's 109,000)

Tsawwassen School Services

  • Delta School District (SD 37)
  • Closure decisions by 6:30 a.m.
  • Website: deltasd.bc.ca
  • School closures are rare due to mild winter weather

Tsawwassen Climate Data (10-Year Average)

  • Annual snowfall: 30-50 cm (12-20 inches) - lowest in Lower Mainland
  • Snow days per year: 6-12 days with measurable snow
  • Days with snow on ground: 15-35 days
  • Coldest month: January (1.5°C average) - warmest winter average in region
  • Heaviest snow months: January, February (light snow typical)
  • Notable: Southernmost location means earliest spring and latest fall temperatures

Report Issues to Tsawwassen/Delta

  • Phone: 311 or 604-946-4411 (Delta)
  • Online: City of Delta website
  • Tsawwassen Ferry: BC Ferries maintains ferry terminal access year-round

Compare the same areas

Put each quote beside one marked site plan. Check entrances, sidewalks, stairs, parking and loading areas individually. A lower headline price may cover fewer areas or a different service trigger. Ask for clarification in writing where a boundary or responsibility is unclear.

Original PlowWow illustrative brand animation. Not a record of a customer job.

Understand the trigger and follow-up

Ask how service is initiated, how ongoing snowfall is handled and what inspection or return visits are included. Separate snow clearing from ice management and hauling. Avoid interpreting a general promise as a specific response time. The written scope should explain what the provider is actually offering.

Identify extra-work decisions

Find out who approves hauling, blocked-access returns or unusual equipment requirements. Ask how charges are communicated before authorizing extra work. Keep your own approval limits and contact arrangements current. This guide is a planning aid; have contractual questions reviewed by an appropriate adviser.

Record practical responsibilities

List who moves vehicles, opens gates, marks obstacles and checks conditions between visits. Share the responsibilities with everyone who needs to act. A signed document alone does not clear a blocked entrance. Review the plan after the first event and keep any agreed changes with the original scope.

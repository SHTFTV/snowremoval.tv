Coordinate Winter Deliveries With Snow Clearing

Published October 18, 2026 · PlowWow editorial

Map the delivery windows

List the times loading bays and service entrances are normally busy. Ask tenants about unusual deliveries before a forecast event. Share the windows with the snow provider so equipment is not repeatedly blocked by vehicles. Keep a contact who can resolve access conflicts available during service.

Original PlowWow illustrative brand animation. Not a record of a customer job.

Make the route visible

Mark turning areas, dock approaches, pedestrian crossings and places where drivers wait. Confirm which surfaces need hand clearing. Snow storage must not consume the space required for a vehicle to turn or for workers to reach a door. Review the route in daylight before the season.

Original PlowWow illustrative brand animation. Not a record of a customer job.

Agree what happens when access is blocked

Specify how the provider reports an occupied area and whether a return visit is included or separately quoted. Avoid assuming that a missed loading bay will automatically be revisited. Ask the delivery coordinator to notify drivers about temporary access changes through their normal communication channel.

Original PlowWow illustrative brand animation. Not a record of a customer job.

Inspect the transitions

Pay attention to the route between the cab, loading area and building. Meltwater, compacted snow and repeated traffic can change conditions after the first clearing. Document the issue and request the appropriate follow-up. Use the service-log guide to keep observations and work records together.
